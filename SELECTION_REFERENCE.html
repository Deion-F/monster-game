🎴 Card Selection Styling Reference

Overview

This document shows the different visual styles applied to cards during various selection contexts in the game. Each context uses a unique color theme to communicate the type of action being performed.

1. Sacrifice Selection (Red/Orange Theme) 🔴

When: Selecting cards to sacrifice for big monsters (Kings, Aces)
Purpose: Indicates cards being destroyed/sacrificed
Visual: Red border, red glow, red-to-orange gradient background
K♠
Q♥
J♦

Try hovering over the middle card to see the selectable state

2. Fusion Candidate Selection (Purple/Magenta Theme) 💜

When: Selecting a hand card to fuse with a field monster
Purpose: Highlights fusion candidates, emphasizes magical fusion
Visual: Purple border, purple glow, animated pulsing effect
10♣
9♠
8♥

Selected cards pulse continuously to show the magical fusion energy

3. Fusion Upkeep Selection (Yellow/Amber Theme) ⚠️

When: Selecting cards to sacrifice for fusion upkeep during End Phase
Purpose: Warning indication of maintenance cost
Visual: Yellow/amber border and glow
7♦
6♠
5♥

Yellow theme communicates cost/warning for maintaining fusions

4. Field Monster Selection (Green Theme) ✅

When: Shows the base monster on field being used for fusion
Purpose: Confirms the fusion target
Visual: Green border, green glow, gentle pulsing animation
A♣
K♥
Q♠

Green confirms which field monster is the fusion base

5. Generic Selection (Primary Purple Theme) 🟣

When: Other selection contexts (trap cancellation, general selection)
Purpose: Default selection indicator
Visual: Primary purple border and glow
4♦
3♣
2♠

Color Legend

Red/Orange: Sacrifice (destruction)
Purple/Magenta: Fusion candidates
Yellow/Amber: Fusion upkeep (cost)
Green: Field selection (confirm)
Primary Purple: Generic selection

Interactive States

All selectable cards show a dashed border hint on hover that matches their selection context color. This helps players identify which cards can be clicked.
